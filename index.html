Your army

Your people’s starting troop is your core: it can reach Lv 8, and the more of your army it makes up, the stronger it gets. Pick up to 4 more lines the draft may offer as supports (one slot from level 3, a second in the Medieval Age). Supports top out at Lv 7, unless they fuse into a union. Stars show each line's mastery: field it in more games to unlock its Lv 7 and 8 forms.

How to play

The rules of the land

  • Lead your general. Tap the map (or use WASD or the thumb joystick) to march; tap an enemy city to attack it. Troops that step inside his circle join his band and fight beside him.
  • Your empire runs itself. Cities grow, settle, build and raise troops on their own. Pick a stance at the bottom, Expand, Build or Defend, to steer where the effort goes.
  • Level up. Science fills the bar at the top. Each level, choose one of three cards: a troop line, an upgrade or a civic. Your troops modernize as the ages pass.
  • Win before the Decline. Four ways: conquest, diplomacy, science or prosperity. The Win tab beside Damage and Goals shows how close you are. At 15:00 the Decline and Fall begins, and at 20:00 the age ends.
  • Stay alive. Barbarian raids, warlords and jealous rivals keep coming. Lose your capital and it is over.
  • Troop lines. Each troop is a line that modernizes from Lv 1 (Ancient) to Lv 8 (Future), at most two ages ahead of your era. Levels 3, 6 and 8 change how it fights, and Lv 4 unlocks its special attack.
  • Core and supports. Your people’s starting troop is your core: cheaper, reaches Lv 8, and stronger when it is most of your army. Support slots open in the Classical and Medieval Ages, up to four lines in all.
  • Counters. Each troop type deals 75% more damage to its prey: Line beats Mounted and Titans, Mounted beats Ranged and Siege, Ranged beats Line and Swarm, and so on. Each card shows its prey. Rivals counter what you field, so mix.
  • Battle lines. Your band forms up facing the enemy: melee in front, archers behind, riders on the flanks, siege and healers at the back. The badly hurt step back to heal. Rivals fight the same way.
  • Orders. Charge (Q), Hold (E) and Fall back (R) for your band. Release (G) sends it to guard a city. Call the Banners (Y) brings every troop on the map to your general. Space fires his ability.
  • Level-up tools. Reroll the three cards, Banish a line so it never returns this game, or Skip for 40 influence (R, B, K).
  • Evolutions and unions. Some lines hide an evolution or a union of two lines. The first time you need its recipe, two pages found in ruins, warcamps and strongholds. After that it is yours in every game.
  • Explore. The map is hidden until you see it. Ruins hold treasure and artifacts (your general keeps up to six), camps hold loot, lost tribes join your band, shrines bless it, and wonders boost you once inside your borders.
  • Borders. Armies may only enter a nation’s land when at war or allied with it. Influence pushes your borders out.
  • Resource sites. Six sites lie between nations: iron, horses, gold, stone, fields and a relic. Stand your troops on one for a few seconds, or let your borders swallow it, to take its bonus. Tap their icons beside Rivals to find them.
  • Outposts. Barbarian warcamps and the Pretender’s keep are heavily guarded. Clear one and walk your general in: elite veterans join your band.
  • Hidden strongholds. From Crown 1, the six hidden peoples wait on the map. They never attack and are very hard to take. Take one and that people is yours for good.
  • The Red Horde. In some games a horde nation rises a few minutes in and raids your cities until you burn the Great Khan’s capital. His hoard is worth it.
  • Stances. Expand puts production into settlers and grows borders fast. Build adds food, science and faster buildings, and speeds starship parts. Defend raises a bigger army that stays home.
  • Decrees. Spend influence for a burst: Mobilize (Z) for damage, Golden Age (X) for yields, Fortify (C) to repair cities and strengthen towers.
  • Governments. Near the start and at every new age, choose one of two governments. Each has a strong perk and a real cost, and the new one replaces the old.
  • Cities and army. Food grows cities. Production pays for troops, up to 90 army room; big troops take more room. You can found 2 cities plus 1 per age; take more by war.
  • Diplomacy. Spend influence on gifts, trade and alliances. Nations lose heart as they lose cities and battles, and a broken nation may surrender.
  • Taking a capital. When a rival’s capital falls, choose: absorb them (their cities, army and troop line join you), make them a vassal (they fight for you and pay tribute), or raze it for a War Chest.
  • Conquest. Take every rival nation by war. Vassals count.
  • Diplomacy. Bring every nation down, with more than half of them joining you by union. A nation joins only if it is your ally, likes you, and has fewer cities than you.
  • Science. From the Industrial Age, with Writing 5 and Astronomy 3, take the Space Program. Pick three launch sites, defend them for at least 4 minutes of building, then hold them all through a 2-minute launch. The Build stance speeds the parts.
  • Prosperity. From the Renaissance, hold 6 cities, 4 resource sites and a wonder inside your borders at once. That starts a Golden Age: keep it for 90 seconds while jealous nations attack. The Expand stance speeds the clock.
  • Losing. Lose your capital, or let a rival launch its starship first.
  • Decline and Fall. If you have not won by 15:00, or you choose to face it after a win, your empire unravels: far cities break away unless your general stands in them, and rebels and barbarians close in on your capital. At 20:00 the End of the Age comes for it. The longer you hold, the more Glory.
  • Glory and Legacy. Every game earns Glory. Spend it in Legacy from the main menu on permanent bonuses.
  • Crowns. Chieftain is for learning, King is Crown 0. Each win opens the next Crown, which adds a twist and pays more Glory, up to Crown 10. Wins also earn a grade, from Bronze to Star.
  • Unlocks. New peoples and troop lines unlock through feats inside a game, wins of each kind, the Crown ladder and hidden strongholds. The Goals tab shows your closest ones; Unlocks on the main menu shows them all.
  • Quests. Every people has three quests in the Collection that pay Glory. Finish all three and that people starts with Lv 2 troops.
